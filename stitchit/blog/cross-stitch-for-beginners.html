Cross stitch for beginners: everything you need to start

Cross stitch is one of the easiest needlecrafts to learn. If you can count and thread a needle, you can make something beautiful this weekend.

By Manuel Couto Updated 15 Nov 2026 7 min read
🪡 🧶 ✨

What you need

  • Aida fabric, 14-count is the easiest to start with. Its grid of holes shows you exactly where to stitch.
  • Embroidery floss, usually DMC. Each skein has 6 strands you separate.
  • A tapestry needle, size 24 for 14-count. It has a blunt tip and a big eye.
  • An embroidery hoop to keep the fabric tight.
  • Small sharp scissors.
  • A pattern: a chart where each square is one stitch and each colour or symbol is one thread.

How to read a cross stitch chart

Each square on the chart equals one X on your fabric. A key tells you which DMC number each colour or symbol stands for. Bold lines every 10 squares help you count.

💡

Start in the middle. Fold your fabric in half both ways to find the centre, and start stitching from the centre of the chart. That way your design won't run off the edge.

How to make your first stitch

  1. Cut about 45 cm (18 in) of floss and separate 2 strands for 14-count Aida.
  2. Come up from the back through the bottom-left hole of a square, leaving a short tail behind.
  3. Go down through the top-right hole. That's half a cross (/).
  4. Come up through the bottom-right hole and go down through the top-left to complete the X.
  5. Secure the tail by stitching over it on the back with your next few stitches. No knots needed.

For rows of one colour, work all the bottom halves (////) along the row first, then come back with the top halves (\\\\). Keep every top stitch going in the same direction; that's what makes the finished piece look neat.

Beginner mistakes to avoid

  • Pulling too tight. It puckers the fabric. Let the thread lie flat.
  • Using all 6 strands. Too thick for 14-count; 2 is standard.
  • Long jumps on the back. Avoid carrying thread more than 3 squares behind open fabric; it shows through.
  • Losing your place. Mark off finished areas on the chart as you go.
  • Picking a huge first project. Start with something under 60 × 60 stitches.

A first project you'll love

Beginners stick with it when the project means something. Make your first one from your own photo: a pet, a plant or a favourite place. StitchIt turns a photo into a pattern with DMC colours, shows how many stitches each colour needs and tracks your completion percentage as you mark off colours.

Frequently asked questions

Is cross stitch easy for beginners?

Yes. It uses one basic stitch on a fabric with a visible grid, so you only need to count squares and follow a chart.

How many strands of floss do I use?

Two strands is standard for 14-count Aida. Use 2 or 3 for 11-count and 1 or 2 for 18-count.

Do I need to tie knots in cross stitch?

No. Secure the thread by stitching over the tail on the back of the fabric. Knots can create lumps and come undone.
