Photo diary

How to keep a photo diary (one photo a day) and actually stick with it

Writing a journal every day is hard. Taking one photo a day isn't. A photo diary gives you a year of memories for ten seconds a day. Here's how to start one and keep it going.

By Manuel Couto Updated 12 Nov 2026 6 min read
🗓️ 📷 🔥

Why a photo diary works

Memory is visual. One picture from a Tuesday in March brings back where you were, who you were with and how you felt, far better than a paragraph of notes. And because a photo takes seconds, you'll actually do it on the days you're tired, busy or not in the mood to write.

How to start

  1. Choose one place for it. Not your camera roll, where diary photos drown among screenshots. Use an album or an app made for it.
  2. Pick a time. Many people choose "the best moment of the day", others "whatever I see at 6pm". Either works if it's consistent.
  3. Add one line. A caption of five words or fewer is enough: "rainy walk, new boots".
  4. Set a gentle reminder. A daily nudge in the evening catches the days you forget.

In Punchie, every photo you punch becomes a stamp that lands on that day in your diary, and a daily reminder keeps your streak alive. Turning each day into a little stamp also makes the diary nicer to flip through than a plain grid.

What to photograph on "nothing happened" days

  • Your lunch, your desk or your shoes
  • The light through a window
  • Something you're reading, listening to or watching
  • A colour you noticed: a red car, a yellow door
  • Your pet doing absolutely nothing
  • The view from your commute

These "boring" days end up being the most precious ones, because they show what normal life looked like.

7 tricks to keep your streak going

  1. Lower the bar. A blurry photo still counts. The streak matters more than the shot.
  2. Attach it to a habit. Morning coffee, the walk home, brushing your teeth.
  3. Make it satisfying. A fun sound or a nice little animation when you save makes you want to come back.
  4. Watch the streak counter. Seeing "9 days" is surprisingly motivating.
  5. Use themes. "Blue week", "food month", "strangers' dogs".
  6. Allow catch-ups. Missed a day? Add a photo you took that day from your library.
  7. Share the recap, not every day. A monthly reel or page is more fun to post and gives you a goal.

Looking back

The magic of a photo diary is the look back. Flip through a month and you'll see patterns: the café you loved, the season you were happiest, the friends you saw most. Punchie's Home Screen widget brings old stamps back on their own, like little memories in your day. You can also turn a month into a scrapbook page or a short reel.

Frequently asked questions

What is a photo diary?

A photo diary is a journal where each day is recorded with one photo, often with a short caption, instead of written entries.

What should I photograph if nothing happened today?

Ordinary details: your lunch, your desk, the light through a window, your commute or your pet. These everyday photos often become the most meaningful ones later.

Is there an app for a one-photo-a-day diary?

Yes. Punchie turns each photo into a stamp that lands on that day in a photo diary, with a daily reminder, a streak counter and a memories widget.
