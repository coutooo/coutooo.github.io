Questions

Good to know.

Can I import birthdays I already have?

Yes — import them from your iPhone Contacts and Calendar, then add anyone missing by hand.

How do I add the widget?

Long-press your home screen, tap the + button, search for B-day Widget and pick a size. It updates automatically as dates get closer.

Does it work for anniversaries?

Yes. Track anniversaries alongside birthdays, each with its own countdown and reminders.

Is B-day Widget free?

It's free to download. B-day Plus (monthly or yearly) and an All Themes pack are optional in-app purchases.
