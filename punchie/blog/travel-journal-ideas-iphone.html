How to make a travel journal on your iPhone

You come home from a trip with 900 photos and no story. A travel journal fixes that, and you don't need a paper notebook or glue stick. Your iPhone is enough.

By Manuel Couto Updated 15 Nov 2026 6 min read
✈️ 📮 🗺️

Why a travel journal beats a camera roll

A camera roll is a pile. A journal is a story: where you went, what you ate, who you met, what made you laugh. It takes a few minutes a day, and it's the one souvenir that gets better with time.

A 5-minute daily routine

  1. Pick 3 photos from today: a place, a plate of food and a detail (a ticket, a sign, a door).
  2. Write one line for each: "best pastel de nata of the trip", "got lost, found this street".
  3. Save them in one place for this trip, not loose in your camera roll.

Do it before bed or over breakfast the next morning. Five minutes, every day of the trip.

12 travel journal page ideas

  1. A cover page: destination, dates and who you travelled with.
  2. The first photo of the trip, from the plane or train window.
  3. "Everything we ate", one stamp per meal.
  4. Doors, windows or street signs of the city.
  5. Tickets: museum, metro, concert, boarding pass.
  6. A map page with every place you visited.
  7. The best view of the trip.
  8. People you met, with their names.
  9. Things that went wrong (the funniest page later).
  10. Local words you learned.
  11. The weather, day by day.
  12. A "last day" page: what you'd do again.

Turn your trip into a book of stamps

Frequently asked questions

How do I start a travel journal on my phone?

Pick one place for the trip, such as an album or a journaling app, and each day save three photos with a one-line caption. Consistency matters more than detail.

What should I put in a travel journal?

Places, food, tickets, people you met, the weather, funny mishaps and a page about what you'd do again.

Can I make a travel scrapbook without printing photos?

Yes. Apps like Punchie let you turn photos into stamps and arrange them on digital scrapbook pages on your iPhone.
