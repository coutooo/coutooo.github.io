Questions

The short version.

Can I use Dinner on my own?

Yes. Solo mode is a quick way to browse what's nearby and shortlist places you like.

How do friends join my session?

Share the session link, or show your QR code and let them scan it. Everyone swipes on their own phone at the same time.

Can I filter the restaurants?

Filter by rating, price level, cuisine and whether a place is open right now.

Is Dinner free?

Dinner is free to download, with an optional premium subscription inside the app.
