The pause that
changes the habit.

Soul Care's detox flow is built on urge surfing — the idea that a craving passes if you let it. Instead of a flat "no," a hard-blocked app opens a short, honest ritual.

  • Breathe while a circle expands and a five-second timer runs.
  • Name what's pulling you back — bored, habit, or avoiding something.
  • Make a conscious choice, and earn vitality for riding it out.
  • Still need in? Unlock for fifteen minutes. No guilt, no judgment.

🔒 Private by design

Soul Care uses Apple's Screen Time API, so all monitoring and blocking happens entirely on your device. Your data never leaves your phone. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.
