How to turn your photos into postage stamps on iPhone

Postage stamps are tiny frames for the things worth remembering. Here's how to turn your own photos into stamps with real perforated edges, right on your iPhone, and what to do with them afterwards.

By Manuel Couto Updated 12 Nov 2026 6 min read
📮 📷 ✨

Why turn photos into stamps?

A camera roll with 20,000 photos is great for storage and terrible for remembering. A stamp is the opposite: one small, framed picture that says this moment mattered. The serrated edge, the white border and the little caption turn a random snapshot into something that feels collectible, and something you actually want to look at again.

Stamps also look great together. A page of stamps from a trip, a month of coffee or every walk with your dog tells a story that a scrolling grid never does.

The quick way: use a stamps camera

The fastest way is an app built for it. In Punchie you look through a stamp-shaped window while you shoot, so you frame the stamp before you take it:

  1. Pick a shape. Portrait, square or landscape.
  2. Pick a style. Classic, full bleed, vintage, airmail, noir or postal, plus a film look such as sunny, dreamy or faded.
  3. Frame and tap PUNCH. You hear a mechanical ka-chunk, feel the haptics, and the photo pops out with perforated edges.
  4. Add a caption. "First coffee in Lisbon", "Bobby's birthday". Short is best.

Already took the photo? Tap Upload, pinch and drag to frame it, and punch it the same way.

The manual way (no app)

If you only need one stamp, you can make it in any design tool:

  1. Crop the photo to a stamp ratio, such as 3:4.
  2. Add a thick white border around it.
  3. Cut evenly spaced semicircles along every edge to fake the perforations. Most editors need a "subtract" or mask tool for this.
  4. Add a small value or caption in a typewriter or serif font, and a soft shadow.

It works, but it takes ten minutes per stamp. If you want to stamp everyday moments, a stamps camera is far less effort.

10 things to stamp first

  • Your morning coffee: a week of cups makes a gorgeous page.
  • Your pet: one stamp per walk, nap or zoomie.
  • Doors and windows on a trip. Colourful doors are made for stamps.
  • The view from wherever you are: a postcard of your day.
  • Food you were excited about: pastries, ramen, birthday cake.
  • Flowers and plants: seasonal and always pretty.
  • Tickets and little paper things before you lose them.
  • The sky at sunset, the same spot every evening.
  • Friends' hands holding drinks, cards, ice creams.
  • One ordinary moment a day. Future you will love these most.

What to do with your stamps

Stamps are more fun in a collection. In Punchie every stamp lands in a stamp book you can open and flip like a real one, and each day shows up in a photo diary. You can also drag stamps onto scrapbook pages, or turn them into a short reel where each stamp punches in on the beat, ready for TikTok or Instagram.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a photo look like a postage stamp?

Crop it to a stamp ratio, add a white border with perforated (scalloped) edges and a small caption. A stamps camera app like Punchie does all of this automatically when you take or import a photo.

Can I turn photos I already have into stamps?

Yes. In Punchie, tap Upload, pick a photo, pinch and drag to frame it, then punch it into a stamp.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Not with Punchie. Stamps, pages and reels are created and stored on your iPhone, with no account and no cloud.
