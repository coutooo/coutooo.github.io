Aida cloth count guide: 11, 14, 16 or 18 count?

The count of your fabric decides how big your finished piece will be, and how easy it is on your eyes. Here's how to choose, and how to do the maths.

By Manuel Couto Updated 15 Nov 2026 5 min read
📏 🧵 🔢

What "count" means

Aida count is the number of stitches per inch (2.54 cm). On 14-count Aida, you make 14 stitches in one inch. Higher count = smaller holes, smaller stitches and a smaller, more detailed finished piece.

11 vs 14 vs 16 vs 18 count

CountStitches per cmStrandsBest for
11~4.33Kids, low vision, big bold designs
14~5.52Beginners and most patterns
16~6.32More detail at a similar size
18~7.11–2Fine detail, photo-realistic portraits

How to calculate the finished size

Use this formula for width and height separately:

Finished size (inches) = stitches ÷ count
Multiply by 2.54 for centimetres.

Example: a pattern 112 × 140 stitches on 14-count is 112 ÷ 14 = 8 in wide and 140 ÷ 14 = 10 in tall, about 20 × 25 cm. The same pattern on 18-count is about 6.2 × 7.8 in (16 × 20 cm).

How much fabric to buy

Add 3 inches (about 8 cm) on every side to the finished size for framing and the hoop. So for the 8 × 10 in example above, cut fabric 14 × 16 in (about 36 × 41 cm).

✂️

Tip: stop the edges fraying with masking tape or a quick zig-zag on a sewing machine before you start.

Plan the size before you convert

When you turn a photo into a pattern in StitchIt, you choose the size in stitches, so you can work backwards from the frame you want. Need a 20 cm wide piece on 14-count? That's about 110 stitches wide.

Frequently asked questions

What Aida count is best for beginners?

14-count is the most common choice for beginners. The holes are easy to see and most patterns are designed for it.

How do I calculate the size of a cross stitch pattern?

Divide the number of stitches by the fabric count to get inches, then multiply by 2.54 for centimetres. Do this for width and height.

How many strands should I use on 18-count Aida?

Usually 1 or 2 strands. Two gives fuller coverage; one gives finer detail.
