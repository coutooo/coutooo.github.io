How to make a monthly photo recap reel

"October in 30 seconds" is one of the easiest and most-loved formats on TikTok and Instagram. Here's how to make one that people actually watch to the end.

By Manuel Couto Updated 15 Nov 2026 5 min read
🎞️ 📮 ✨

1. Collect through the month

The best recaps are made in small pieces. Save one or two photos a day as you go, in a photo diary or a dedicated album. At the end of the month you'll have 30 to 60 moments to choose from instead of digging through 1,000 photos.

2. Pick 15 to 25 and put them in order

  • Open strong. The first photo decides whether people keep watching. Use your most surprising or beautiful shot.
  • Mix it up. Alternate wide shots, close-ups, people and food.
  • Keep it chronological for the middle; it tells a story.
  • End on a feeling. A sunset, a hug, a quiet moment.

3. Time it to the music

Recaps work best when each photo changes on the beat. A good rhythm is 0.3 to 0.6 seconds per photo, slowing down for one or two hero shots. 15 to 25 photos gives you a reel of roughly 8 to 15 seconds, short enough that people watch it twice.

5 ideas to make yours stand out

  1. A consistent frame or border on every photo, like a stamp, so the reel feels designed.
  2. A date or caption on each frame.
  3. "One photo per day" exactly, so the reel has 28 to 31 frames.
  4. A theme: "every coffee I had in March".
  5. A final frame with the whole month as a grid.

Frequently asked questions

How many photos should a photo dump reel have?

Around 15 to 25 photos is a good range, giving a reel of about 8 to 15 seconds.

How long should each photo be on screen?

About 0.3 to 0.6 seconds, changing on the beat of the music, with a slightly longer pause on one or two key shots.

What is a monthly recap?

A short video or post that sums up your month in photos, usually in chronological order, set to music.
