How to block apps on iPhone: Screen Time and better options

Your iPhone can already block apps and websites with Screen Time. Here's exactly how to set it up, why the built-in limits are easy to ignore, and what to do if you keep tapping "Ignore Limit".

By Manuel Couto Updated 15 Nov 2026 6 min read
🛡️ 📵 ⏰

Option 1: Screen Time App Limits

  1. Open Settings → Screen Time and turn it on if it isn't already.
  2. Tap App Limits → Add Limit.
  3. Pick a category (Social, Entertainment, Games) or expand it to choose individual apps.
  4. Set a daily time, for example 30 minutes, and optionally customise it by day.
  5. Turn on Block at End of Limit.

When you hit the limit, the app icon dims and opening it shows a "Time Limit" screen.

Option 2: Downtime for whole periods

Downtime blocks everything except the apps you allow, for a time window you set. It's great for nights.

  1. Go to Settings → Screen Time → Downtime.
  2. Turn on Scheduled and set the window, for example 22:30 to 07:00.
  3. In Always Allowed, keep Phone, Messages and anything you genuinely need.

Option 3: Block websites in Safari

Go to Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → App Store, Media, Web & Games → Web Content, choose Limit Adult Websites, then add any site under Never Allow. This is useful for the browser version of an app you've already limited, which is a common loophole.

The problem: "Ignore Limit" is one tap away

Unless someone else sets a Screen Time passcode for you, the limit screen offers Ignore Limit → 15 minutes or Ignore for Today. In the moment, you'll tap it. Most people do, which is why limits often stop working after the first week.

Blocks that stick tend to have one of these:

  • A real pause before you can get in, long enough to break autopilot.
  • A schedule tied to your routine (bedtime, morning, work), not a vague daily total.
  • Something at stake, so that ignoring the block costs you something you care about.

Frequently asked questions

Can I block an app completely on iPhone?

With Screen Time you can set a one-minute App Limit with Block at End of Limit, or use Downtime. To stop yourself bypassing it, ask someone else to set the Screen Time passcode, or use an app with hard blocking modes.

How do I block apps only at certain times?

Use Screen Time Downtime for a single daily window, or an app like Soul Care that lets you schedule different blocks for bedtime, mornings, work and weekends.

Does blocking apps delete my data?

No. Blocking only stops the app from opening. Your data, messages and settings stay exactly as they were.
