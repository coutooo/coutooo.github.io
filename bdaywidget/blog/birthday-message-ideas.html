What to write in a birthday card: 40 birthday message ideas

You've got the card, the pen and nothing to say. Here's a simple formula, and 40 messages you can use as they are or tweak to make your own.

By Manuel Couto Updated 15 Nov 2026 6 min read
💌 🎉 ✍️

The 3-part formula

  1. Greeting: "Happy birthday, Sam!"
  2. Something specific: a memory, a quality you love or something they did this year.
  3. A wish for the year ahead: "Here's to more road trips."

The specific part is what makes a message feel personal. Even one detail beats a long generic poem.

For friends

  • Happy birthday to the person who makes every plan better.
  • Another year of you being the funniest person I know. Lucky us.
  • Thanks for always picking up the phone. Happy birthday!
  • Here's to more late dinners and terrible decisions together.
  • You're getting older, but our jokes are not getting any better.
  • Happy birthday! Let's celebrate properly this weekend.
  • Cheers to the friend who's basically family.
  • I'd say "don't grow up", but it's a bit late. Happy birthday!
  • Thanks for another year of adventures. Can't wait for the next one.
  • May your coffee be strong and your year even better.

For family

  • Happy birthday, Mum. Everything good in me started with you.
  • Dad, thanks for every lift, every lesson and every bad joke.
  • To my favourite sibling (don't tell the others). Happy birthday!
  • Grandma, your stories are my favourite gift. Happy birthday.
  • Happy birthday to the person who taught me how to [ride a bike / cook / drive].
  • Wishing you a year as warm and generous as you are.
  • However old we get, you'll always be my hero. Happy birthday.
  • So lucky to call you family. Have the best day.
  • Happy birthday! Dinner's on me this time.
  • Thank you for always being there. Today, it's all about you.

For your partner

  • Every year with you is my favourite year so far.
  • Happy birthday to my favourite person to do nothing with.
  • You make ordinary days feel like holidays.
  • I'd choose you again, every single time.
  • Here's to another year of inside jokes and slow mornings.
  • Thank you for being my home. Happy birthday, love.
  • I still get excited every time you walk in. Happy birthday.
  • Let's make this year our best adventure yet.
  • You + me + cake. Perfect day.
  • Happy birthday to the one who makes me laugh the hardest.

For colleagues

  • Happy birthday! Thanks for making Mondays bearable.
  • Wishing you a fantastic day and a great year ahead.
  • Happy birthday to the best desk neighbour.
  • Thanks for all the help this year. Enjoy your day!
  • Hope your birthday is as great as your spreadsheets.
  • Wishing you a relaxing day, with no meetings.
  • Happy birthday! The team is lucky to have you.
  • Cheers to another year of great work and good coffee.
  • Have a wonderful birthday and a well-deserved break.
  • Happy birthday! Cake in the kitchen at 3?

Frequently asked questions

What do you write in a birthday card?

Start with a greeting, add something specific about the person, such as a memory or a quality you love, and finish with a wish for the year ahead.

How long should a birthday message be?

Two to four sentences is plenty. One specific, personal detail matters more than length.

What do you write in a colleague's birthday card?

Keep it friendly and simple: thank them for something specific and wish them a great day and year ahead.
