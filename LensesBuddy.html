Questions

Quick answers before you download.

Which types of lenses does it support?

Daily disposables, two-weekly, monthly and longer-wear soft lenses. You set the duration, so any replacement schedule works.

Can I track each eye separately?

Yes. LensesBuddy keeps a usage history for each eye and lets you manage several lens types and prescriptions at once.

Is there a home-screen widget?

Yes — add the widget to see the days left and a progress bar at a glance, without opening the app.

Is LensesBuddy free?

It's free to download. Full access to every tool is available with a monthly or yearly subscription inside the app.
