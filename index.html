How I actually work.

I build the whole thing. Idea, design, code, App Store listing, landing page, support inbox — there's no handoff, because there's no one to hand off to.

Most of what I ship starts as a small personal annoyance. A contact-lens reminder I kept forgetting. A grocery list my house kept losing. A screen-time habit I wanted to break. Then it becomes an app, and sometimes other people want it too.

  • 01Ship small, ship often. Seventeen App Store releases in twenty-four months, and four in a single quarter.
  • 02Solo end to end. Every app on this page was designed, built, and published by one person.
  • 03Privacy first. Most of these apps keep data on-device — no accounts, no cloud.
  • 04Research background. MSc work on blockchain-based authentication for NDN, plus V2X vehicular networking.
