Modes

Four ways to climb

Same tap, same tower — four very different kinds of pressure. The harder the mode, the more it pays.

Mode 01

Classic

The original. The block bounces wall to wall and the speed ramps steadily. This is the one you'll chase your best score in.

Standard payout
Mode 02

Rush

Everything moves faster from the very first block. No warm-up, no easing in — just reflexes from block one.

1.5× coins
Mode 03

Precision

You start on a block half the usual width. There's no margin to trim away, so every placement has to be near-perfect.

1.6× coins
Mode 04

One Way

One pass per block, no bounce. Let it reach the far wall unplaced and the run is over — slower, but there's no second chance.

2× coins

How it works

Three seconds to your first tower

Step 01

Pick a mode

Classic, Rush, Precision or One Way — each keeps its own best score, so every mode is a fresh record to break.

Step 02

Tap to drop

Centre it for a PERFECT, close for a GOOD, anything else trims the overhang. Miss the tower entirely and the run ends.

Step 03

Cash in

Coins land per block, per combo milestone and per level-up, then go straight into the shop for your next skin or scene.
