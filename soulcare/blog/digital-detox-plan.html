Digital detox

A 7-day digital detox plan you'll actually finish

Most digital detoxes fail because they start with "no phone for a week". This plan goes the other way: one small, specific change each day, so by day seven you've rebuilt how you use your phone without a dramatic cold turkey.

By Manuel Couto Updated 15 Nov 2026 6 min read
🌿 📵 ✨

Before you start: get your baseline

Open Settings → Screen Time → See All App & Website Activity and write down two numbers: your daily average and your top three apps. You'll compare them at the end of the week. Don't judge the number, just note it.

The 7-day plan

  1. Day 1: Notifications. Turn off notifications for every app that isn't a person talking to you. Keep calls, messages and calendar.
  2. Day 2: The Home Screen. Move social and video apps off your first page. Put useful tools there instead: maps, notes, a book app.
  3. Day 3: Phone-free mornings. No feeds for the first 30 minutes after you wake up. Coffee, light, a walk, anything else.
  4. Day 4: Bedtime block. Block feed apps from an hour before bed. Charge the phone outside the bedroom if you can.
  5. Day 5: Focus blocks. Do two 45-minute sessions of work or study with distracting apps blocked.
  6. Day 6: An offline afternoon. Leave the phone at home for a few hours: a walk, a café, friends, a museum.
  7. Day 7: Review and keep. Compare your numbers with day zero. Keep the two changes that felt easiest. Those are your new defaults.

Tips that make it stick

  • Tell someone. A friend doing the week with you doubles your odds.
  • Expect the itch. The urge to check is strongest on days 2 and 3, then fades.
  • Plan replacements. Boredom is the trigger. Have something ready: a book, a podcast, a sketchbook.
  • Don't go to zero. The goal is control, not abstinence. Your phone is still useful.

Make the plan automatic

Days 3, 4 and 5 are much easier when your phone enforces them for you. Soul Care has presets that match this plan almost exactly: Mindful Morning, Bedtime, Work Focus and Weekend Detox. Turn one on each day, and watch your Soul grow as the screen time drops.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a digital detox be?

A week is enough to notice a difference and build new habits. Shorter detoxes help too; even a phone-free afternoon each weekend makes a difference over time.

Do I have to delete social media for a digital detox?

No. Removing apps from your Home Screen, turning off notifications and blocking them at certain times usually works better long-term than deleting accounts.

What should I do instead of using my phone?

Plan specific replacements: reading, walking, cooking, calling a friend, a hobby. Boredom is what sends you back to the phone, so have an alternative ready.
