Questions

Is Punchie free?

Punchie is a premium app. You can start with a free trial and cancel any time from your Apple ID settings before it ends — we'll even remind you the day before. There's also a one-time lifetime option.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Every stamp, page and reel is created and stored on your iPhone. There's no account and no cloud. If you delete a stamp, it's gone.

Can I use photos I already have?

Yes — tap Upload in the punch, pinch and drag to frame, and punch it. The camera switches off while you do, so it doesn't drain your battery.

Can I add my own pictures and stickers to scrapbook pages?

Yes. Add regular photos, stamps, 100+ stickers in themed packs, washi tapes, words and text — or make a sticker from any photo with Cut-out.

Which devices are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
