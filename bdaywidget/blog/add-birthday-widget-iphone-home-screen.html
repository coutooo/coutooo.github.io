How to add a birthday widget to your iPhone Home Screen

The best birthday reminder is one you see without looking for it. A widget puts the next birthdays right on your Home Screen, every time you unlock your phone.

By Manuel Couto Updated 15 Nov 2026 5 min read
📱 🎂 ⏳

Why a widget works better than notifications

A notification arrives once, on the day, often when you're busy, and it's gone with a swipe. A widget shows you what's coming up, days in advance, every time you unlock your phone. That's what gives you time to plan a gift or a message.

Add it to your Home Screen

  1. Install a birthday widget app and add your birthdays (or import them from Contacts).
  2. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  3. Tap Edit in the top corner, then Add Widget.
  4. Search for the app and choose a size: small for the next birthday, medium or large for a list of upcoming ones.
  5. Tap Add Widget, drag it where you want it and tap Done.

Add it to your Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen, then tap Customize → Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the widget area under the clock.
  3. Pick the birthday widget and tap Done.

Tip: use a Smart Stack

Short on space? Drag one widget on top of another of the same size to create a stack. iOS can rotate to your birthday widget automatically when a birthday is close, so it appears exactly when you need it.

Frequently asked questions

Does iPhone have a built-in birthday widget?

The Calendar widget can show birthdays from Contacts, but only alongside other events. A dedicated birthday widget app shows countdowns to upcoming birthdays.

Can I put a birthday countdown on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Customise your Lock Screen, tap the widget area under the clock and add a birthday widget.

Which widget size is best?

Small shows the next birthday; medium and large show a list of upcoming birthdays, which is better for planning ahead.
