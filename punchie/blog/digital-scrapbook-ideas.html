Scrapbooking

15 digital scrapbook ideas to keep your memories on iPhone

Scrapbooking without scissors, glue or a craft drawer. These 15 ideas work on any iPhone and take minutes, not afternoons. Most of them work best with photos turned into little stamps.

By Manuel Couto Updated 12 Nov 2026 7 min read
✂️ 📒 🌼

Before you start

A good digital scrapbook page has three things: a theme (one trip, one month, one person), a few strong images rather than 40 so-so ones, and little extras such as washi tape, stickers and a handwritten-style caption. Keep each page to 4–8 photos and it will always look intentional.

Turning photos into small stamps first helps a lot: they're consistent in size and framing, so even a busy page looks tidy. (Here's how to turn photos into stamps.)

Everyday pages

  1. The coffee diary. One stamp per cup for a week or a month. It's surprisingly beautiful.
  2. The monthly recap. Pick your 6 favourite moments of the month and add one word under each.
  3. "Things that made me smile". Small joys only: a good sandwich, a funny sign, a free afternoon.
  4. Seasons. The same tree, park or window in spring, summer, autumn and winter.
  5. A recipe page. Ingredients, the finished dish and who you cooked it for.

Travel pages

  1. One page per city. A door, a dish, a view and a ticket.
  2. The "first time" page. First swim of the year, first snow, first time abroad.
  3. A map of stamps. Place stamps roughly where they happened on a blank page and add arrows.
  4. Postcards home. A stamp with a two-line note, as if you'd mailed it to a friend.
  5. Weekend away. Friday, Saturday and Sunday in three rows.

People & pets

  1. Birthday page. The cake, the people, the card. Add the date big.
  2. A year of your pet. One stamp a month to watch a puppy grow up.
  3. Friendship page. Inside jokes as captions, guaranteed to make you laugh in five years.
  4. "Us" page. Dates, trips and silly moments with your partner.
  5. Family recipe or tradition. The people behind a dish you'll want to remember.

Layout tips that make pages look great

  • Pick a paper first. Cream, kraft or a grid instantly sets the mood.
  • Rotate a few items slightly. 2–6 degrees feels hand-made; perfectly straight feels like a template.
  • Overlap on purpose. Tuck one stamp under a strip of washi tape.
  • Use one sticker theme per page. Mixing everything gets noisy fast.
  • Leave white space. Empty paper is what makes the photos pop.

Frequently asked questions

What is a digital scrapbook?

A digital scrapbook is a page made on your phone or computer that combines photos with paper backgrounds, stickers, tape and text, like a paper scrapbook without the glue.

How many photos should a scrapbook page have?

Between 4 and 8 usually looks best. Fewer, stronger photos with some empty space look more intentional than a crowded page.

Can I make a scrapbook on my iPhone?

Yes. Apps like Punchie let you place stamps, photos, stickers, washi tape and text on paper backgrounds and export the page as an image for stories or posts.
